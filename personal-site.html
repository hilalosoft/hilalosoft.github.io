About

This page is designed as a compact personal profile rather than a full CV. It highlights the overlap between doctoral research, full-stack development, and hands-on technical problem solving. The emphasis is on work that is careful, reproducible, and useful.

What I work on

Empirical software engineering PHP & Laravel React & Next.js Python analysis Docker & Linux Requirements & systems design

My background combines software evolution research, backend and frontend development, technical writing, and data-driven investigation. I care about traceability, maintainability, and explaining complex systems clearly.

How I approach problems

I tend to work from evidence first: reproduce the issue, inspect the system, compare alternatives, then refine the solution. That applies equally to code, infrastructure, academic analysis, and product decisions.

Outside work, I am deeply interested in board games, game systems, PC gaming, language learning, and practical product research. Those interests feed a design style that values clarity, strategy, and usable detail.

Research interests

I am especially interested in the boundary between software engineering theory and implementable systems: requirements engineering, maintainability, metrics, software evolution, and development processes that can be validated rather than hand-waved.

  • Empirical studies of software change and quality.
  • Structured digital requirements and traceability.
  • Human-centered systems in constrained institutions.
  • Tooling that supports both research rigor and practical adoption.
